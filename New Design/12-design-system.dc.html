One column count per band: 4 for metrics, 5 for the digest, 3 for KPIs and sessions, 2 for patterns. Bands are separated by a full-bleed 1px rule and never by a gap; inner divisions use the same rule so the page reads as one sheet, not a set of cards.
Rules of the direction
DONumbers carry hierarchy — a bigger number, not a heavier card.
DOEvery label is mono, uppercase, tracked; every value is sans and tabular.
DOCharts are 1–2px strokes on a 5-line grid, no fills, no gradients.
NORounded corners, drop shadows, glass blur, glow.
NODonuts and pies — share is always a bar or a stacked rule.
NOEmoji, coloured backgrounds, more than three accents on a page.
Rollout · what each screen becomes
Timeline
Focus calendar band, then the log as a ruled table — time, app, title, duration — no cards per row.
Activity
Active/idle columns full width, intensity bubbles become a ruled scatter with mono axes.
Power
CPU list keeps the bar row; power impact map becomes a plotted grid, watts as the big metric.
Tools
New page: Pomodoro as one large tabular timer, goals and breathing as ruled bands beneath.
Settings
Two-column ruled list: label left, control right, sections divided by the same 1px rule.
